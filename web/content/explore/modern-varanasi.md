Varanasi is changing fast. New infrastructure, redevelopment and growing visitor numbers are reshaping parts of the city, while its ancient rhythms continue alongside.

## Major projects

- The **Kashi Vishwanath Dham corridor**, inaugurated in **December 2021**, opened up the area around the [Kashi Vishwanath Temple](/places/temples/kashi-vishwanath) and created a direct route between the temple and the river.
- New riverfront spaces such as [Namo Ghat](/places/ghats/namo) have been developed in the north of the city.
- Roads, transport links and other public works have expanded. [VERIFY: current major projects and their status — Government of India / Uttar Pradesh press releases]

## Tourism today

Visitor numbers have grown strongly in recent years, with very large crowds at major festivals and on auspicious days. [VERIFY: latest visitor figures — Uttar Pradesh Tourism] That brings opportunity for local people, but also pressure on the old city's lanes, the ghats and the river.

## Balancing growth and heritage

Redevelopment has changed the look of some areas, and debates continue about how best to protect the city's heritage, its residents and its environment while welcoming visitors. Responsible travel helps: travel in the quieter seasons where you can, support local businesses and artisans, keep the ghats and river clean, and respect sacred spaces.
