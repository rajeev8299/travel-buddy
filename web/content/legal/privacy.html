What we collect

When you send an enquiry or booking request

  • Your name, email and/or phone number, and how you'd like us to contact you.
  • Trip details: dates, number of travellers, the service or experience you're interested in, and anything you write in your notes — including any health, mobility or dietary needs you choose to tell us.
  • If you send a plan from the trip planner, the text of that plan.
  • What happens next: the quotes we send you, whether you accept or decline them, the partner assigned to your trip, and the messages we send you about it.

Please only tell us about health or other sensitive matters if it helps us plan your trip safely. If you send a request for someone else, make sure they're happy for you to share their details.

When you apply to be a buddy

  • The application form: name, contact details, date of birth, languages, experience, two references' names and phone numbers, and the type of identity document you hold with only its last 4 digits — we never ask for or keep the full number.
  • If you're shortlisted, through your private verification link: a government photo ID, a recent photo of you, a police verification certificate and, if you have one, a guide licence; the name and short introduction travellers will see; the area where you live; an emergency contact; and your availability.
  • Our checks: whether each document was accepted, who checked it and when, and notes from reference calls and your interview.

When you write for our blog

Authors' names, photos, short biographies, areas of expertise and any links they choose to add appear on their public author page and on their articles. We also keep a record of who wrote, edited, checked and approved each article.

Automatically

  • Your IP address is used briefly to stop abuse, such as too many requests in a short time. We don't store it with your enquiry.
  • On your own device: the trip planner and "My trips" save your plans in your browser's storage, and the site briefly remembers your private booking link and the page you came from so the enquiry form works smoothly. This stays on your device — clear your browser data to remove it.
  • Usage counts: to learn which pages and tools help people, the site records simple events — for example "page viewed", "itinerary generated (2 days)" or "quote responded" — with the page, the website you came from and any campaign tag in the link. These records contain no name, contact details, IP address or anything you typed, and aren't linked to you.
  • Cookies: staff, partner and author accounts use one sign-in cookie. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies, and we don't use third-party analytics.

Why we use it

  • To answer your enquiry, prepare a quote, arrange your trip with a buddy or partner, and support you on the day (you asked us to — this is the purpose you consent to on the form).
  • To contact you about your request and trip by email, phone or WhatsApp. We don't send marketing messages unless you separately agree to them.
  • To verify buddy applicants — checking documents, calling references and interviewing — so travellers can trust the people we introduce.
  • To keep the website secure, prevent fraud and spam, and meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

  • The buddy or partner who provides your service (for example a guide, boat operator or driver) receives only what they need: your name, contact number, dates, group size, meeting point and relevant needs.
  • Travellers see a buddy's public name, introduction and photo — never their documents, address, references or emergency contact.
  • Service providers who run parts of the service for us — website and database hosting, email delivery and WhatsApp messaging[[, payment processing]] — under contracts that limit their use of your data. Some may process data outside India, with safeguards the law requires.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it or to protect someone's safety.

How we protect it

  • Private links: your "My booking request" page and a buddy's verification page open from a private link. We store only a scrambled (hashed) form of the link's secret, links expire (booking pages after 60 days, verification pages after 14 days), and we can switch a link off at any time. Don't forward your link — anyone who has it can see that page.
  • Buddy documents are encrypted as soon as they reach us and can be opened only by our verification team. They are never shown to travellers or partners.
  • Staff accounts have only the access their role needs, and important actions are logged.

How long we keep it

  • Buddy documents: each file is deleted as soon as our team has checked it. Files that are never checked are deleted after 30 days. We keep only a note of which document was checked, when, by whom and the result.
  • Enquiries and bookings, including quotes and your acceptance: [[e.g. 3 years]] after your trip or after the enquiry closes, for accounting, tax and support.
  • Buddy applications that don't go ahead: [[e.g. 6 months]] after the decision. Approved buddies' records are kept while they work with us and for [[e.g. 3 years]] after.
  • Accounts: until you ask us to close them, then as needed for the records above. Published articles keep their author credit.
  • Message records (what we sent you, when and whether it was delivered): [[e.g. 1 year]].

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data we hold about you and who we've shared it with;
  • correct or update it;
  • delete it, unless we must keep it by law;
  • withdraw your consent — this doesn't affect what we did before, but we may not be able to continue a trip or application without the data;
  • name someone to exercise these rights for you if you die or can't act yourself.

Write to our Grievance Officer below. We'll reply within [[e.g. 30 days]]. If you're not satisfied, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Children

Enquiries should be sent by an adult. We don't knowingly collect data from children except what a parent or guardian tells us about a child travelling with them. Buddy applicants must be 18 or over.
