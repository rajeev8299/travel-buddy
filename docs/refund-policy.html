We want cancelling to be simple and fair. This policy applies to experiences booked with Registered business name ("TravelOnBuddy").

If you cancel

  • 48 hours or more before the start time: full refund.
  • Between 48 and 24 hours before: 50% refund, or move to another date at no charge (once).
  • Less than 24 hours before, or if you don't show up: no refund.

If we cancel or change your experience

  • River closures: boat rides don't run when the Ganga is in flood or the administration suspends boating. We'll offer a ghat walk or another date, or refund you in full — your choice.
  • Weather, curfews or site closures: same choice — a different date, an alternative experience, or a full refund.
  • If your buddy doesn't show: we refund that day in full and find you another buddy the same morning wherever we can.

How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the original payment method within 7 working days of being approved. Bank or card processing can add a few days.

How to cancel

Email hello@travelonbuddy.in or message us on WhatsApp at WhatsApp number with your booking reference. The time we receive your message is the time we use for the rules above.
