Good to know

  • Crowds are very heavy on festival days — arrive early and keep valuables close.
  • When the river is high the Aarti moves up the steps; boat viewing may not be possible.
  • Dev Deepawali, Ganga Dussehra and big festivals fill up fast. Book well ahead.
