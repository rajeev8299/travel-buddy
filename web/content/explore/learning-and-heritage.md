For centuries Varanasi has been a city of learning. Scholars, students and teachers have come here to study Sanskrit, philosophy, grammar, astronomy and the sacred texts, and that tradition continues today.

## A centre of scholarship

**Tradition and history** both describe Kashi as a place where scholars gathered and debated. Pandits taught students in homes and temple schools (*pathshalas*), and the city became known for the depth of its Sanskrit learning. ## Universities

- **[Banaras Hindu University](/explore/banaras-hindu-university)**, founded in 1916 by Pandit Madan Mohan Malaviya, is one of India's major universities.
- **Sampurnanand Sanskrit University** continues the tradition of Sanskrit learning. [VERIFY: founding history — university website]
- **Mahatma Gandhi Kashi Vidyapith**, founded during the independence movement, is home to the [Bharat Mata Temple](/places/heritage/bharat-mata-temple). [VERIFY: founding year — university website]

## Living culture

Learning in Varanasi isn't confined to universities. Music is taught from teacher to student in long-established traditions, crafts are passed down in families, and religious discourse fills temples and ghats.
