This policy explains what personal data Registered business name ("TravelOnBuddy", "we") collects through this website, why, and the choices you have. We follow India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Who we are

Registered business name, Registered address, Varanasi, Uttar Pradesh, India. GSTIN: GSTIN. Contact: hello@travelonbuddy.in.

What we collect

  • Trip requests: your name, email and/or phone number, dates, group size, the area or experience you're interested in, and anything you choose to tell us in your notes.
  • Accounts: name, email, phone number, home city or company, and a securely hashed password. We never store your password itself.
  • Buddy applications: the details on the application form, including date of birth, the type and number of an identity document, and two references' names and phone numbers.
  • Technical data: a single sign-in cookie when you log in. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

Why we use it

  • To answer your enquiry, match you with a buddy and plan and run your trip.
  • To contact you about your trip by email, phone or WhatsApp.
  • To verify buddy applicants and check their references, so travellers are safe.
  • To keep the website secure and to meet legal and tax obligations.

We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

  • The buddy assigned to your trip, who receives only what they need to meet and guide you.
  • Service providers who run parts of the service for us — website hosting, email and WhatsApp messaging, and (when added) payments — under contracts that limit their use of your data.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Trip requests and bookings: e.g. 3 years after your trip, for accounting and support. Buddy applications that are not accepted: e.g. 6 months. Accounts: until you ask us to delete them.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal data, or withdraw your consent, by writing to our Grievance Officer below. We will reply within e.g. 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.
