## Status: none of these are publishable yet

Every page has `status: draft`, `last_verified: null` and `reviewer: null`. A page may only move to **Published** once three things are done:
1. Every `[VERIFY: …]` placeholder has been replaced with a value confirmed at the source it names.
2. A fact-checker has worked through `09-verification-checklist.md` for the page and recorded their sources.
3. A local cultural reviewer has read every section about religious practice, cremation or etiquette.

## Labelling conventions

| Label | Meaning | How it appears on the live site |
|---|---|---|
| **Documented history** | Supported by inscriptions, dated records, published scholarship or official bodies such as the ASI or a temple trust | Plain statement |
| **Archaeological evidence** | Findings from excavation or dating of structures | Plain statement, naming the evidence |
| **Tradition & belief** | Religious narratives, legends and devotional belief | Always introduced with "According to tradition…", "Devotees believe…" or "Legend says…". Never written as fact. |
| **Suggested** | Our editorial recommendation, such as a visit duration or the best time of day | Labelled "Suggested" |
| `[VERIFY: what at where]` | A time-sensitive or uncertain fact that must be confirmed before publishing. No value is guessed. | Must never appear on the live site. The CMS refuses to publish a page that contains this marker. |

## What these pages never contain

- Opening hours, Aarti times, entry fees, queue times or "VIP darshan" arrangements that haven't been checked against an official source.
- Distances, fares or travel times stated as fact.
- Business names, ratings, endorsements or partner claims.
- Encouragement to photograph cremations, or any framing of cremation as a spectacle.
- A position on matters before the courts.

## Sources to check (shared)

These are the starting points for verification. Each page lists its own as well. Confirm the current addresses at verification time.

- Shri Kashi Vishwanath Temple Trust, official website and notices
- Archaeological Survey of India (ASI): Sarnath monuments and Sarnath Museum
- Uttar Pradesh Tourism, official website
- Varanasi district administration and Varanasi Police, for traffic, river and festival advisories
- Varanasi Municipal Corporation / Smart City notices
- Published scholarship, for example Diana L. Eck, *Banaras: City of Light* (1982), which is a standard reference for the city's history and traditions
