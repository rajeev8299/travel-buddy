Good to know

  • Boats don't run when the Ganga is in flood (usually in the monsoon). We'll swap in a ghat walk and tell you in advance.
  • No photos at the cremation ghats — your buddy will tell you where it's fine.
  • Winter mornings on the river are cold; bring a shawl or jacket.
