# Varanasi Trip Companion — Product & Content Specification

> *Discover the Soul of Varanasi. Plan Smarter. Travel with Confidence.*

This folder holds the product and content specification for **Varanasi Trip Companion**, a
mobile-first travel-planning and local-experiences platform for Varanasi, Uttar Pradesh. Its
strategic foundation is the *Business Feasibility, Market Analysis and Financial Plan* (v1.0,
2 August 2026).

**These are planning documents, not website content.** Nothing in `product/` is published:
the public site lives in `docs/`. Sample pages in `08-sample-content/` are drafts. Every
`[VERIFY: …]` item in them must be checked against the named source before it goes live.

## Ground rules used throughout

1. **Facts, traditions and suggestions are labelled differently.**
   - *Documented history* and *archaeological evidence* are stated as fact, with sources.
   - *Tradition & belief* is presented as what devotees believe or what tradition holds.
   - *Suggested* (routes, durations) and *Estimate* (costs, travel times) are labelled as such.
2. **No invented operational facts.** Opening hours, fees, queue times, prices, distances,
   phone numbers, festival dates and partner verification statuses appear only after
   verification, with a "Verified on" date and a review or expiry date.
3. **Knowledge pages and commercial pages stay distinct.** Guides are neutral. Partner
   offerings and services are clearly marked and use tracked enquiries. There's no checkout in
   the MVP.
4. **Cultural respect.** Sacred sites and cremation ghats are described with care. Photography
   guidance puts people's privacy first.
