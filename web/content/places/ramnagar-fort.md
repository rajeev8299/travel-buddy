# Ramnagar Fort

On the eastern bank of the Ganga, across the river from the ghats of Varanasi, stands **Ramnagar Fort**. It is the ancestral seat of the **Kashi Naresh**, the hereditary Maharaja of Benares. Built in sandstone and overlooking the river, it combines a royal residence, a museum of the family's collections and a temple. It is also the heart of one of India's most remarkable living traditions, the month-long **Ramnagar Ramlila**.

## Introduction and significance

Ramnagar offers a different view of Varanasi. Here you see the city's royal and courtly history, and you look back across the water at the crescent of ghats. For many visitors, the fort's quiet courtyards and the riverside view are as memorable as the museum.

The **Kashi Naresh** holds a revered place in the city's religious life. Many residents traditionally regard the Maharaja as a representative of Shiva, and greet him with "Har Har Mahadev". The royal family still uses part of the fort as a residence.

- **Riverfront walls and bastions** in cream-coloured sandstone, with balconies and pavilions overlooking the Ganga. The view back towards the ghats is one of the best reasons to come.

## Rules: what to carry and what to avoid

- **Respect private areas.** The royal family lives in part of the fort, so keep to the visitor routes.
- **Follow the museum's rules** on photography and bags.
- **Carry water and sun protection.** The approach and courtyards can be hot.
- **During the Ramlila**, the audience follows the performance between locations around Ramnagar. Wear comfortable shoes, carry a torch and be ready for crowds.

## Frequently asked questions

**Who lives in Ramnagar Fort?**
The fort is the ancestral home of the Kashi Naresh, the hereditary Maharaja of Benares. Part of it is still a private residence.

**What is in the Ramnagar Fort museum?**
The royal family's collections. Commonly reported highlights include vintage cars, palanquins, arms, costumes and an astronomical clock. Displays can change. [VERIFY]

**What is the Ramnagar Ramlila?**
A month-long open-air performance of the Ramayana held in and around Ramnagar under the patronage of the Kashi Naresh. It is one of the best-known Ramlila traditions in India.

**When does the Ramlila take place?**
Over about a month in the autumn, around Dussehra. Exact dates change every year. We only publish them once they are officially announced.

**Can I see Ramnagar Fort from a boat?**
Yes. Its riverside walls are visible from the Ganga, especially from boats near Assi.
