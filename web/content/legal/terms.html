  2. We send you a quote with the price, what's included and not included, and who provides it. A quote is valid for [[e.g. 7 days]] unless it says otherwise, and may change if your dates or group change.
  3. You accept or decline the quote on your booking page or by replying to us. Accepting asks us to confirm it with the buddy or partner; it isn't a booking yet.
  4. Your booking is confirmed only when we tell you in writing — with the date, time, meeting point and price — and your booking page shows Confirmed.

If we can't confirm (for example the partner isn't available), we'll tell you and offer another option. Nothing is charged for a booking we couldn't confirm.
