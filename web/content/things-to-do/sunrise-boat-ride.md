# Sunrise boat ride on the Ganga

At first light, the river is where Varanasi comes alive. Bathers step into the water, priests begin their prayers, yoga groups gather on the steps, and the old buildings along the ghats glow as the sun rises over the far bank. A sunrise boat ride is the calmest way to take it all in, and for many visitors it becomes the defining memory of their trip.

## Who it's for

- **First-time visitors**, as the best introduction to the ghats
- **Photographers**, for soft light and river views (please read the etiquette below)
- **Families and seniors**, who will see the riverfront without walking the long ghat chain
- **Anyone short on time**: an hour on the water covers many ghats

## What you'll see

The exact route depends on where you start and how long you go. A typical ride from the south passes:
- **Assi Ghat**, with its morning programme of music and Aarti on many days (`/places/ghats/assi`)
- **Tulsi, Kedar and Harishchandra Ghats**. Harishchandra is a cremation ghat: please observe it quietly.
- **Dashashwamedh Ghat**, busy with pilgrims and priests (`/places/ghats/dashashwamedh`)
- **Manikarnika Ghat**, the principal cremation ghat: please observe it respectfully and don't take photos (`/places/ghats/manikarnika`)
- **Scindia, Panchganga and beyond**, if the ride continues north

## Rowing boat or motor boat?

| | Rowing boat | Motor boat |
|---|---|---|
| Atmosphere | Quiet; the traditional experience | Noisier |
| Distance covered | Shorter, slower | Longer stretch of ghats |
| Group size | Usually private, small groups | Often larger, sometimes shared |
| Best for | Couples, photographers, a calm first morning | Covering more ghats, larger groups |

**Where to start:**
- **Assi Ghat** is the most popular starting point. It's easy to reach by road and good for a ride north along the whole ghat chain.
- **Dashashwamedh Ghat** is central, and good if you're staying in the old city.
- A ride ending near **Dashashwamedh or Manikarnika** lets you continue on foot to the Kashi Vishwanath corridor for morning darshan.

## Safety

- **Wear a life jacket** if one is offered, and ask for one before you set off. [VERIFY: current regulations on life jackets]
- **Don't stand up or move around** while the boat is moving, and keep your hands inside when passing other boats.
- **Boarding and getting off** means stepping onto a moving boat from wet steps. Take the boatman's hand.
- **In fog** (common on winter mornings), visibility can be very low. Follow the boatman's judgement on whether to go.
- **Never take a boat during floods** or when the authorities have suspended boating.

## Etiquette

- **Cremation ghats:** do not photograph or film cremations, mourners or bodies, not even from the water. Pass quietly.
- **Bathers:** don't photograph people bathing or changing.
- **Rituals:** keep your voice low near people at prayer, and don't use flash.
- **Offerings:** if you'd like to float a *diya* (leaf-cup lamp), buy it from a vendor on the ghat before boarding.

## What to bring

- A shawl or jacket in winter, because river mornings are cold
- A small amount of cash or UPI for chai afterwards
- Your camera, with the etiquette above in mind
- Water, and a cap for longer rides

## Frequently asked questions

**What time should I start a sunrise boat ride?**
About 30 minutes before sunrise (Suggested). The time changes through the year, so check the forecast for your date.

**Do boats run during the monsoon?**
Not when the Ganga is in flood. Boating is usually suspended for part of the monsoon. Check the current status on **Today in Varanasi** (`/travel-guide/today`).

**Rowing or motor boat?**
A rowing boat is quieter and more atmospheric. A motor boat covers more ghats. See the comparison above.

**Is a sunrise boat ride suitable for elderly travellers?**
Yes, as long as you take care getting on and off. Ask the boatman to steady the boat and help you.

**Can I take photos?**
Yes, of the ghats, the light and the river. But please don't photograph cremations, mourners or people bathing.

**Sunrise or sunset ride?**
Sunrise shows morning rituals and soft light. Sunset rides often end with the evening Ganga Aarti at Dashashwamedh (`/things-to-do/sunset-boat-ride`).
