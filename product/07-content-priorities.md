# 7. Content Priorities and Production Plan

**Product:** Varanasi Trip Companion
**Positioning:** *Discover the Soul of Varanasi. Plan Smarter. Travel with Confidence.*
**Status:** Product specification v1.0, 1 November 2026. Tags match [01-sitemap.md](01-sitemap.md): **[MVP]** Phase 1, **[P2]** Phase 2, **[F]** Future.

How priority was decided:
- What the feasibility plan says Phase 1 must prove: itinerary use, partner enquiries, and content checked on schedule.

- Safety value.

## 7.3 Production order across the 12-week plan

The content batches follow the execution plan agreed earlier: go live and pilot, then verified content, then partners and leads, then the planner, then measurement and live notes, then trust and launch. Each batch is ready just before the software or operations work that depends on it.

| Weeks | Execution plan focus | Content batch | Pages |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1–2** | Go live, start the concierge pilot | **A. Safety & pilot essentials:** Home, Today alerts, emergency, temple access rules, how to reach, getting around, last-mile walking, scams & touts. Also the trust and legal pages, outside the count. | 8 (+9) |
| **3–4** | Verified content system | **B. River & top temples:** places hub, temples hub, Kashi Vishwanath, Sankat Mochan, Durga Kund, Kaal Bhairav, New Vishwanath; ghats hub, Dashashwamedh, Assi, Manikarnika, Harishchandra; Things to Do hub, sunrise and sunset boat rides, Ganga Aarti, morning ghat walk; best time to visit, weather & monsoon, food & water safety | 20 |
| **5–6** | Partners & tracked leads | **C. Services & experiences:** Services hub and all 18 MVP service pages; Local Experiences hub; heritage walks, temple circuits, food walks, silk weaving visits. The first partner offerings go live here. | 24 |
| **7–8** | Itinerary planner | **D. Itineraries & Sarnath:** Plan hub, itineraries hub, 6 itineraries; heritage hub, Sarnath, Dhamek Stupa, Sarnath Museum, Ramnagar Fort; nearby hub; Sarnath Buddhist tour; Buddhism & Jainism; families & seniors; solo & women safety | 18 |
| **9–10** | Measurement & live notes | **E. Food, shopping & festivals:** Food & Shopping hub, food hub + 6, shopping hub + 2, markets hub + 3; festivals hub, Dev Deepawali, Kartik Purnima, Mahashivratri, Holi | 20 |
| **11–12** | Trust & launch | **F. Explore & remaining guides:** Explore hub + 8 articles; Travel Guide hub, airport, railway stations, responsible travel, FAQ | 14 |
| | | **Total** | **104** |

The total is one more than the §7.2 count because Home is counted in batch A.

**About the ordering:**
- The festival pages in batch E publish in **weeks 9–10, about 6 weeks before Mahashivratri and Holi**. Their dates go up only once officially confirmed.
- Dev Deepawali falls in the current season, before these pages ship. During the pilot, the ops team answers Dev Deepawali questions directly, and the page is ready well ahead of next year's festival.
- Batch C depends on partner onboarding. Service pages can publish with "Enquire. Our team will match you with a verified partner" before every category has listed partners.
- Explore articles come last because they are evergreen, need the most research, and their absence doesn't stop anyone planning a trip.

## 7.4 Weekly targets

These are cumulative. "Verified" means the page has passed Fact Verification and is publishable.
- **Time-sensitive pages publish only once verified.**
- Evergreen pages publish after editorial review and fact-check of their historical claims.

|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 4 | 2 | Emergency and Today pages live for the pilot |
| 2 | 8 | 8 | Batch A complete; trust and legal pages live (once business details are filled in) |
| 3 | 18 | 12 | |
| 4 | 28 | 20 | **20 verified pages** (execution plan target) |
| 5 | 40 | 30 | |
| 6 | 52 | 40 | Every service category live |
| 7 | 61 | 49 | |
| 8 | 70 | 58 | Itineraries live alongside the planner |
| 9 | 80 | 66 | |
| 10 | 90 | 75 | **60+ verified pages** (execution plan target exceeded) |
| 11 | 98 | 88 | |
| 12 | 104 | ≥ 99 | **≥ 95% of MVP pages verified**, in line with the feasibility plan's "95%+ time-sensitive content verified on schedule" target |

**Capacity this assumes:**
- About 10 drafts a week from 2 writers.
- One local reviewer and one fact-checker clearing about 9 pages a week.
- From week 6, the fact-checker also re-verifies weekly items (temple rules, alerts) and monthly items (emergency, partners).
- If verification falls behind by more than one batch, **slow drafting, not verification**.

## 7.5 Phase 2 order (months 4–9)

These are sequenced by season and by the evidence from the month-6 decision gates:

1. **Before the monsoon:** accessibility, what to pack, money & UPI; refresh the monsoon guidance; Hindi for temples, emergency and how to reach.
2. **Before Sawan:** the Sawan festival page, plus Hindi for the spiritual itinerary and temple rules.
3. **Before the Ramlila and Dev Deepawali season:** Ramnagar Ramlila, Nag Nathaiya, Ganga Mahotsav, the events calendar, and a Dev Deepawali refresh.
4. **Ongoing, in batches of about 10 a month:** remaining ghats and temples, heritage sites, Explore depth articles, food and shopping long-tail pages.
5. **Once lead conversion and partner reliability meet the decision gates:** stays, outstation transport and partner profiles.
6. **Blog:** launches once at least 2 real, consented traveller stories exist.
