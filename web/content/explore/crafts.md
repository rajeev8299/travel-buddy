Varanasi is a city of makers. Alongside its famous [silk weaving](/explore/banarasi-silk), the city's workshops produce a wide range of traditional crafts, many of them made by families who have practised them for generations.

## Crafts to look for

- **Wooden toys** — brightly lacquered toys and figures, a traditional craft of the city. [VERIFY: GI status of Varanasi wooden lacquerware and toys — Geographical Indications Registry]
- **Brass and metalwork** — lamps, bells and ritual vessels used in temples and homes, traditionally sold in areas such as [Thatheri Bazaar](/markets/thatheri-bazaar).
- **Glass beads** — beadwork used in jewellery and decoration. [VERIFY: GI status — Geographical Indications Registry]
- **Stone carving and inlay** — small carved pieces and souvenirs.
- **Musical instruments** — tabla, sitar and other instruments, reflecting the city's [musical tradition](/explore/music-arts-literature).

## Buying crafts responsibly

- Buy directly from makers or cooperatives where you can, so more of the price reaches the artisan.
- Ask how and where an item was made.
- Be wary of shops recommended by people who earn commission on your purchase.
