We want cancelling to be simple and fair. This policy applies to trips, services and experiences arranged through Varanasi Trip Companion, run by [[Registered business name]]. If your quote sets out different terms for a particular service (for example a train ticket or hotel booked on your behalf), those terms apply to that part — we'll always point them out before you accept.

Before your booking is confirmed

  • Sending a request or receiving a quote costs nothing. You can decline a quote on your booking page or simply not accept it.
  • If you've accepted a quote but we haven't yet confirmed your booking, you can change your mind for free — just tell us.
  • If we can't confirm your booking, anything you've paid is refunded in full.

If you cancel a confirmed booking

  • [[48]] hours or more before the start time: full refund.
  • Between [[48]] and [[24]] hours before: [[50]]% refund, or move to another date at no charge (once).
  • Less than [[24]] hours before, or if you don't show up: no refund.

For multi-day trips, these limits apply to each day's services separately, counted from the start of that day's first service.

Changing your booking

Want a different date, time or group size? Ask as early as you can. If we can make the change, we'll send an updated quote; there's no charge for a change made [[48]] hours or more before the start time, as long as the price is the same. A smaller group may change the price.

If we or a partner cancel or change your experience

  • River closures: boat rides don't run when the Ganga is in flood or the administration suspends boating. You choose: a ghat walk or other alternative, another date, or a full refund for that part.
  • Weather, curfews or site closures: the same choice — another date, an alternative, or a full refund.
  • Buddy or partner can't make it: we'll try to find you a replacement of the same standard straight away. If we can't, or you'd rather not, you get a full refund for that part.
  • We change something significant (the date, the start time by more than [[2]] hours, or the main activity): you can accept the change or cancel for a full refund.

If a service runs but is shortened because of our or a partner's fault, we'll refund the part you missed.

When there's no refund

  • You arrive late or leave early — though we'll always try to make the most of the time left.
  • The experience is ended because a traveller behaved in a way that put others at risk.
  • Costs you paid directly to third parties, such as entry tickets, offerings or shopping.

How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the original payment method within [[7]] working days of being approved. Bank or card processing can add a few days. If you paid a partner directly, we'll arrange the refund with them and keep you updated. We'll tell you the amount and the reason in writing.

How to cancel or ask for a refund

Email hello@travelonbuddy.in or message us on WhatsApp at [[WhatsApp number]] with your reference code. The time we receive your message is the time we use for the rules above. We'll confirm the cancellation and any refund in writing.
