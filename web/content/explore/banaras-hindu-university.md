**Banaras Hindu University (BHU)** is one of India's best-known universities and one of the largest residential universities in Asia. [VERIFY: "largest residential university" claim — BHU] For visitors, its green, spacious campus in the south of the city is a calm contrast to the old city.

## Documented history

BHU was founded in **1916** by **Pandit Madan Mohan Malaviya**, a leader of the independence movement and an educator. Annie Besant's Central Hindu College became part of the new university. [VERIFY: details of the founding and the role of the Central Hindu College — BHU official history]

## What to see

- **[New Vishwanath Temple](/places/temples/new-vishwanath-bhu)** — a spacious Shiva temple at the centre of the campus, built with support from the Birla family.
- **[Bharat Kala Bhavan](/places/heritage/bharat-kala-bhavan)** — the university museum, with collections of miniature paintings, textiles and sculpture.
- The wide, tree-lined avenues of the campus itself, designed around a semicircular plan. [VERIFY: campus layout description — BHU]

## Visiting

The campus is a working university, so be considerate of students and staff. Vehicle access and opening hours for the temple and museum are set by the university. [VERIFY: campus access rules for visitors — BHU] Combine a visit with the nearby temples at [Durga Kund](/places/temples/durga-kund) and [Sankat Mochan](/places/temples/sankat-mochan).
