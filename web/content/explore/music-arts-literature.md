Varanasi has given India some of its greatest musicians, poets and writers. Music in particular is woven into the city's life, from temple bells to concerts that last all night.

## Music

The **Banaras gharana** is one of the great schools of Hindustani classical music, especially known for **tabla** and for vocal forms such as *thumri*. [VERIFY: description of the Banaras gharana — published musicology sources]

Among the many musicians associated with the city:

- **Ustad Bismillah Khan**, the shehnai maestro, lived in Varanasi and was awarded the Bharat Ratna, India's highest civilian honour, in 2001.
- **Pandit Ravi Shankar**, the sitar virtuoso, was born in Varanasi in 1920.
- **Girija Devi**, the celebrated singer often called the "queen of thumri", was from Varanasi.

## Literature

- **Kabir**, the 15th-century poet-saint whose verses are loved by Hindus, Muslims and Sikhs alike, is closely associated with Varanasi.
- **Tulsidas**, author of the *Ramcharitmanas*, lived and wrote in the city in the 16th century; [Tulsi Ghat](/places/ghats/tulsi) is named after him.
- **Munshi Premchand**, one of the greatest writers of modern Hindi and Urdu fiction, was born near Varanasi in 1880.
